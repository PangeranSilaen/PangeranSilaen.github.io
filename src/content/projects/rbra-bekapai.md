## Overview

RBRA Bekapai is the official digital profile website for *Ruang Bermain Ramah Anak* (Child-Friendly Play Space) at Taman Bekapai, a central public park in Balikpapan. Delivered as part of a university community service initiative (KKN), the project aimed to provide visiting families and city residents with verified information regarding public park safety standards, child play facilities, and park rules.

## Community & Environmental Context

Taman Bekapai is an urban hub in Balikpapan with high weekend foot traffic. However, families often lacked clear information regarding which zones were age-appropriate for toddlers versus older children, emergency contact protocols, and park cleanliness guidelines.

## Technical Execution

The website was designed with a mobile-first philosophy, prioritizing rapid load times over cellular data networks. Built using Next.js with static site generation (SSG), pages load near-instantaneously on mobile devices. Visuals were created in Figma and structured for accessible typography, high contrast, and intuitive navigation for citizens of all digital literacy levels.
