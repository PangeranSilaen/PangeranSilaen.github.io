## Overview

PermataKiddo is an academic information management system built specifically for the operational needs of TK Permata Bunda. Unlike generic academic systems built for secondary schools or universities, early childhood education requires recording developmental milestones, fine motor progress, physical health measurements, and teacher observations alongside standard tuition records.

## Real-World Operational Needs

- **Preschool Assessment Structure:** Standard numerical grading doesn't apply to kindergarten. PermataKiddo models narrative developmental indicators across cognitive, social-emotional, language, and physical domains.
- **Tuition & Operational Fees:** Automated generation of monthly school fee invoices, manual bank transfer proof upload, and accounting validation dashboards for the school treasurer.
- **Parent Communication:** Parents receive verified milestone updates, event notices, and attendance records directly without sorting through unstructured group chat messages.

## Technical Execution

Built on Laravel and Filament with a PostgreSQL backend, the system enforces strict relational models between academic years, class cohorts, student profiles, and developmental rubric indicators. PDF report cards can be generated in batch for semester parent-teacher meetings, cutting compilation time from weeks of handwriting to minutes.
